Our mission

The purpose of Aim See Succeed is to foster an environment that inspires athletes, students, parents, and the community to create opportunities that allow youth in Central Pennsylvania to invest in themselves and their futures while enriching local programs. Our main goal is to find ways to alleviate the roadblocks and obstacles for youth in our area so that they can focus on becoming better athletes, better students, and better people. We plan to support community youth by providing them with the equipment they need, sponsoring participation in camp and training opportunities, and providing transportation to outside events that would enhance their skills while providing them with opportunities and experiences that they would otherwise not be able to participate in. We aim to ensure that the youth in our area have all of the support they need to succeed in anything they decide to do to build a brighter future for themselves and our community.

Rooted in Central Pennsylvania

Our mission brings athletes, students, parents, and the community together around youth opportunity and stronger local programs.
